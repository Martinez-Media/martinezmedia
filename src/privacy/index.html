Privacy, in context.

This page covers this website and the specific WaveWrangler behavior described below. It is not a blanket privacy policy for every product or self-hosted deployment.

Updated November 17, 2026

This website

The Martinez Media website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, account registration, contact forms, or embedded third-party media. Fonts, styles, and images are served with the site. There is no client-side application code required to use it.

Your browser still makes requests to the hosting provider. GitHub Pages may collect technical information, including IP addresses, for security and operation of the service. See GitHub’s privacy statement for the hosting provider’s practices. “No site analytics” does not mean “no hosting logs.”

When you contact us

Email links open your email application; this website does not submit a form. If you send a message, your address, message, and any attachments are processed by email providers and received by Martinez Media so we can respond. Do not send passwords, private recordings, or other sensitive material in an initial inquiry.

For a privacy question or a request concerning information you’ve emailed us, write to contact@martinezmedia.net.

Links to other services

GitHub repositories and issues, social profiles, and linked product sites are separate destinations with their own practices. GitHub issues are public. Self-hosted deployments depend on their operator’s configuration and any integrations they enable.

WaveWrangler: local media and transcription

WaveWrangler is in active development. Based on its current product documentation, original recordings are referenced in place rather than rewritten. Editing decisions are stored in the local show document.

Whisper inference runs locally on your Mac. Model downloads happen only when requested. Recordings and transcripts are not uploaded for transcription. A model download is a network request; local transcription is not a claim that the app never accesses the network.

If you choose to keep recordings or documents in a cloud-synced location, that provider’s behavior is separate. A verified local save does not confirm that cloud syncing has completed. Third-party Audio Units, software, codecs, and models have their own terms and behavior.

This is a statement about the documented media and transcription workflow, not an assertion about every third-party component or a future App Store privacy disclosure. See the current WaveWrangler documentation and saving and recovery guide.

Other products

Meal Planner, its MagicMirror integration, WeatherWrangler, and Print Pantry are not covered by the WaveWrangler statement. Consult their source and documentation, and the operator of any deployment, before entering personal data or enabling integrations. Find each project.
