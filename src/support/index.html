A useful bug report
starts small.

Tell us the product and version, your operating system, what you tried, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Share the smallest steps that reproduce the problem. Screenshots can help, but remove personal information first.

GitHub issues are public. Don’t upload private recordings, transcripts, family details, credentials, or confidential files. Use synthetic examples where possible. Email us first if a report needs private context.
