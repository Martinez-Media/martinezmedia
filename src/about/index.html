We’re Martinez Media, LLC. An independent software company founded in 2011, with a history in photography, web design, and helping people bring something into the world.

It started with
people and their stories.

Brandon had been doing freelance web and design work since 2002, as a teenager. By 2011, the volume justified making it official. Brandon and Joy Martinez formed Martinez Media, LLC that February in Ludington, Michigan.

From 2011 to 2023, our client-services work was primarily photography: weddings, families, and high-school seniors around West Michigan. Alongside it came websites, branding, print design, and social-media consulting for local businesses.

That period is part of our story, not the end of the company. Martinez Media remains active, with a focus on building software.

Now, we’re making
tools of our own.

WaveWrangler brings separately recorded podcast tracks into one conversation. Meal Planner helps a family decide what’s for dinner. WeatherWrangler brings the weather into the next outing. Print Pantry organizes the things you want to make.

They’re different projects with a common starting point: a practical task, and a reason to make doing it better.

We publish source and documentation so you can see the work for yourself. Availability and licensing vary by project; we keep those distinctions visible.
